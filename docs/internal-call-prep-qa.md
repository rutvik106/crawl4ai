# Client Call Prep — Q&A Cheat Sheet (INTERNAL ONLY)

**Purpose:** Justify pricing and handle objections on the TPL/Torrent call.
**Do not share this document with the client.**

---

## Pricing Objections

### Q: "Rs. 12 lakh license is too high. Why?"

**A:** "The platform represents 6+ months of specialized development — AI pipeline, anti-bot crawling, regulatory milestone detection. Comparable enterprise tools like AlphaSense or Citeline cost Rs. 8–20 lakh per year just for subscriptions, without custom sources or your report format. You're getting a dedicated, tailored system at a one-time price. And you can de-risk it entirely with the Rs. 1.5 lakh pilot — fully credited if you proceed."

### Q: "Why Rs. 1–1.5 lakh/month just to run it?"

**A:** "It's all-inclusive: cloud hosting, AI processing (every article is analyzed by large language models — that has real per-article cost), proxy infrastructure to access protected sites, and 24x7 monitoring with daily delivery. Think of it as replacing several hours of analyst time daily — the fee is a fraction of one analyst's salary."

- Do NOT break down actual costs. If pushed: "It's a bundled managed-service fee; itemizing doesn't apply since we absorb usage fluctuations."

### Q: "Why is the range Rs. 1–1.5L? Lock a number."

**A:** "The tier depends on how many sources you want and how often we crawl. After we finalize your source list in onboarding, we lock the number in writing. It won't change without your approval."

### Q: "Why AMC on top of monthly fees? Isn't that double charging?"

**A:** "No — the monthly fee runs the platform (infrastructure, AI, delivery). AMC covers engineering upkeep: websites redesign constantly and break crawlers; we fix them proactively so your reports never stop. Different work, different team function."

### Q: "Rs. 18k/dev-day for changes — everything costs extra?"

**A:** "Bug fixes and source repairs are free under AMC. The dev-day rate only applies to new capabilities. Every request gets a written estimate first — you approve before we start. You control the spend completely."

## Trust / Capability Questions

### Q: "Is this proven? Who else uses it?"

**A:** Lean on the track record: live since Feb 2026, 6 months of continuous operation, changes typically shipped within days. If there is no other client, don't lie — say "it's been in live production operation and iterated with real users for 6 months," and offer the pilot as proof.

### Q: "Why do you need 3–4 more months if it's already built?"

**A:** "The platform is fully functional today — you get reports from week one. The 3–4 months is tuning to you: your sources, your analysts' judgment on relevance, your format. Any intelligence system needs calibration to its user; we're upfront about it instead of pretending it's plug-and-play."

### Q: "What if AI misses important news or gets things wrong?"

**A:** "We built a never-drop policy — items are demoted, not deleted, so nothing silently disappears. Every report item links to its source URL so your team can verify. During onboarding we tune scoring against your analysts' feedback."

### Q: "Data security? Where is our data?"

**A:** "Single-tenant deployment — your data is never mixed with anyone else's. All reports and data remain your property contractually. Enterprise security hardening (audit logs, credential vaulting) is part of the month 3–4 roadmap." Be honest here; pharma will probe.

### Q: "What if you disappear / stop supporting it?"

**A:** Have an answer ready: source-code escrow, or a handover clause on termination. Offering this proactively builds huge trust for a small vendor.

## Negotiation Strategy (INTERNAL)

- **Your floor (do not reveal):** room on license down to ~Rs. 9–10L and monthly fee down to ~Rs. 80k while staying profitable. Concede slowly, and trade — e.g., lower license for annual advance payment or a 2-year commitment.
- **First concession should never be price** — offer the pilot, extra training, or an extra source instead.
- **If they push hard on monthly:** offer Rs. 1L/month locked for year 1 with a defined source cap.
- **Anchor on their cost:** "One CI analyst costs Rs. 15–25L/year. This platform is a fraction of that and never takes a day off."
- **Close on the pilot** if they hesitate: "Let's not debate the full commitment — run the 3-month pilot, judge the output, and the fee credits against the license."
